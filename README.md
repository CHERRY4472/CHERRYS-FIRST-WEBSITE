# Adithya Traders & Spices website

## Files
- `index.html` — website content, business name, image links and contact details
- `style.css` — responsive styling and design

## Before publishing
1. Open `index.html` and search for `91XXXXXXXXXX`. Replace it with your WhatsApp number including country code, digits only (example format: `919876543210`). Replace every occurrence.
2. Replace `Location to be added` and `Update this section...` with your actual business location.
3. The displayed photos are illustrative photos from Unsplash, not photos of your own packaged product. Replace the image URLs in `index.html` with your own product-photo filenames or trusted image URLs if you have them.
4. Check that all business descriptions accurately reflect your products and services.
5. Product prices are not shown until you confirm them; use the contact links to ask for current prices.

## Publish to the existing GitHub Pages website (phone)
1. Download and extract `adithya-traders-website.zip`.
2. Open GitHub in your browser and sign in.
3. Open your existing repository: `cherry4472/Chilli-powder-website-`.
4. Open `index.html`, tap the pencil/Edit button, replace its contents with the new `index.html`, then choose **Commit changes**.
5. Repeat for `style.css` using the new `style.css`.
6. Wait a minute or two, then open:
   https://cherry4472.github.io/Chilli-powder-website/
7. If the old design remains, refresh the page or open it in a private/incognito tab.

Important: Save copies of your current files before replacing them. Do not share your GitHub password or verification codes.
